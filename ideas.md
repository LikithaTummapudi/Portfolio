## Chosen Approach — Field Notes / Editorial Technical

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design informed by Swiss International Typographic Style and the visual language of research notebooks, technical papers, and annotated datasets.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence over spectacle:** let the work, contribution details, and progression carry the story.
2. **Editorial hierarchy:** use oversized display type, compact metadata, and deliberate whitespace to create a readable rhythm.
3. **Measured technicality:** use data-inspired lines, nodes, coordinates, and index labels as quiet framing devices.
4. **Warm precision:** pair paper-like neutrals with one ownable blue accent so the interface feels human and exact.

### Color Philosophy
The base is a soft paper tone rather than stark white, supporting a thoughtful, personal reading experience. Near-black ink provides strong contrast and seriousness. Cobalt blue is reserved for links, active states, data marks, and emphasis so it reads as a signal color rather than decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-rail section index and offset editorial blocks create a guided vertical reading path. Content alternates between wide narrative bands and asymmetrical project/experience arrangements; on small screens the rail collapses into a compact top navigation.

### Signature Elements
- A persistent cobalt section index that marks the current chapter while scrolling.
- A minimal neural/data-node constellation in the hero, drawn with CSS and SVG rather than stock imagery.
- Small uppercase metadata labels and ruled dividers that make each section feel like a page in a research log.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning a page or inspecting a dataset: links underline cleanly, cards lift slightly, navigation indicates the current chapter, and no interaction competes with the content.

### Animation
Reveal sections with a short opacity/translate transition as they enter the viewport. Stagger only grouped items. Use a slow, subtle drift for hero data nodes and a restrained count-up for highlight statistics. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing all non-essential motion.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for display headings and labels, with **DM Sans** for body copy and UI text. Headings are large and tightly tracked; supporting text remains relaxed and readable. Monospaced snippets use the system monospace stack only for technical metadata.

### Brand Essence
A thoughtful AI/ML-focused computer science undergraduate building practical software and learning in public through projects, collaboration, and contribution.

Personality: **curious, precise, grounded**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and specific. CTAs are invitations to inspect the work, not inflated promises. Microcopy sounds observant and quietly confident.

Example lines:
- “Learning by building systems I can explain.”
- “Explore the work behind the signal.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact “LT” monogram built from two offset vertical strokes and a connecting crossbar, evoking a data point joining a path. It is used as a graphic mark beside the full name rather than replacing the wordmark.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Cobalt — #2457D6**. It is bright enough to guide attention, but controlled enough to preserve the editorial, research-led tone.

## Style Decisions
- Keep the page light, warm, and editorial; avoid generic SaaS hero layouts.
- Use one restrained accent color only: Signal Cobalt.
- Treat technical motifs as annotations, not decoration.
- Never use fake terminal windows, skill bars, fake testimonials, or unsupported claims.
- Use a left index on desktop and a concise mobile menu on smaller screens.

## Revision Direction — Warm Editorial Technical

The revised site keeps the research-log structure but shifts the visual foundation to **cream, beige, tan, dark brown, and muted caramel** at the user’s direction. The accent is intentionally warm rather than cobalt. Technical identity is sustained through data plots, coordinate-style annotations, a persistent desktop field index, ruled records, and evidence-frame placeholders. These blank project and certificate frames are intentional replacement points for user-provided images, not stock imagery.

## Structured Revision Direction

The portfolio now favors a **structured, recruiter-readable card system** over asymmetric compositions. The visual rhythm comes from alternating cream, beige, and dark-brown sections; strong Manrope headings paired with occasional DM Serif emphasis; consistent radii, spacing, tags, and micro-interactions; and one memorable hero character illustration. Creative detail belongs inside coherent grids, polished cards, subtle reveals, and image placeholders—not scattered layouts.
